# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance, and relationships between numerical variables.

## Objective
The objective is to analyze retail sales data and uncover useful patterns, customer behavior trends, and actionable business insights.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 1,000 retail transactions with information about:
- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset inspection and data quality checks
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Quarterly sales analysis
- Customer gender distribution
- Customer age-group analysis
- Product category sales volume
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation analysis using a heatmap
- Gender-wise sales analysis

## Key Insights
- Sales varied considerably across different months.
- May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales among the complete months.
- The customer distribution was nearly balanced by gender.
- The 46–55 age group had the highest number of customers.
- Clothing had the highest quantity sold.
- Electronics generated the highest revenue among the three product categories.
- Female customers generated slightly higher total sales than male customers.

## Business Recommendations
1. Maintain sufficient stock for high-performing product categories.
2. Plan promotions and inventory around monthly sales trends.
3. Use customer demographics to support targeted marketing campaigns.
4. Monitor pricing and quantity relationships to improve inventory and pricing decisions.
